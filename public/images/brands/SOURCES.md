# Brand asset sources

- Technology SVGs: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), original variants. Devicon's artwork is distributed under MIT; brand marks remain owned by their respective owners.
- ESPRESSIF and MQTT: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 distribution; brand marks remain owned by their respective owners.
- UPC: original red logo from the header of [Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas](https://www.upc.edu.pe/), saved without modifying its artwork. Source: https://upc-cdn.b-cdn.net/web-institucional/static/img/logo_upc_red.png. Used to identify the university listed in the portfolio's academic record.
- JC signature: original vector created for Julio Castro's portfolio.
